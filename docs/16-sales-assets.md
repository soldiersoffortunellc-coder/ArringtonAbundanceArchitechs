## Standing rules across every asset

1. Never invent a statistic, testimonial, or commitment not sourced from
   the client, approved config, or this repo.
2. Never present a modeled/projected figure as a guaranteed result.
3. Never quote unapproved pricing.
4. Every asset that touches insurance or real estate carries the relevant
   compliance note from its industry-pack doc.
