# Final Three-Industry Recommendation

## Recommendation

Build and sell the **Insurance Agency Revenue OS**, **Real Estate Revenue
OS**, and **Home-Services Revenue OS** as the three Phase 1 industry packs,
confirming the brief's mandate. All three clear the 48+/60 bar on the
Market Opportunity Scorecard (`docs/01-market-opportunity-scorecard.md`);
no industry outside this set is built until Day 90+.

## Why these three together (not in isolation)

They are complementary, not redundant, on the factors that matter for a
90-day cash sprint:

| | Insurance | Real Estate | Home Services |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fastest to close | No — FMO/compliance layer slows decisions | Medium | **Yes** — owner-operator decides same call |
| Highest expansion ceiling | Multi-agent recruiting network | Team/brokerage multi-seat | Multi-location/franchise |
| Best recruiting-fee upside | **Yes** — licensed-agent recruiting funnel | No | No |
| Best near-term retention | Medium | Lower (commission-cyclical) | **Highest** (essential infra, low switching) |
| Sales-cycle length | Longest | Medium | **Shortest** |

Running all three in parallel means the shortest-cycle vertical
(home services) fills the pipeline and produces early proof points and
case studies while the longer-cycle, higher-ceiling verticals (insurance
recruiting, real estate teams) are worked in the background and close
mid-to-late in the 90 days.

## Recommended internal sequencing (not exclusion — sequencing)

1. **Home Services** — first demos, first closes, first case study. Fund
   the Days 15–30 validation phase.
2. **Real Estate** — second wave; large addressable market and high
   referral density accelerates Days 31–60 sales acceleration.
3. **Insurance** — worked in parallel from Day 1 but expect later closes
   given the FMO/compliance sales cycle; recruiting-fee economics justify
   the extra cycle length.

## Compliance note

Insurance carries the heaviest compliance load (state insurance regulation,
E&O, TCPA/consent for recruiting outreach). Per the brief's rule —
*"Do not sacrifice compliance, delivery quality or client retention for
short-term sales"* — the Insurance industry pack ships with explicit
**Compliance review controls** (`docs/06-industry-pack-insurance.md`) and
no insurance client goes live without that checklist signed off.

## What activates next after Day 90

Per the Days 61–90 objective *"Decide which secondary industry should be
activated next,"* the top two candidates from the secondary screen are
**property management** and **med spas** (see
`docs/01-market-opportunity-scorecard.md`). The actual decision should be
made only after Phase 1 delivery margins and case studies are confirmed —
this is a placeholder recommendation, not a Day-90 commitment.
