## PAGE 2: ABOUT

**SEO Title:** About Coach Shonough | Veteran-Led Financial Leadership & Legacy Building
**Meta Description:** From Cavalry Scout to financial executive — the story of Coach Shonough's journey from military service to building the Open Doors Ecosystem.

---

### Hero

# From the Battlefield to the Boardroom

**Subhead:** Discipline doesn't retire when the uniform comes off. It just finds a new mission.

---

### Body Copy

I go by Coach Shonough — but most people know the story starts with Rashon Arrington.

I served as a Cavalry Scout in the U.S. Army, deployed in support of Operation Iraqi Freedom, and carry the Combat Action Badge from that service. The Army taught me things no classroom could: how to move under pressure, how to lead people who are counting on you, and how to stay in the fight long after the adrenaline wears off. Those lessons didn't stay in uniform — they became the foundation for everything I've built since.

When I came home, I saw the same gap show up again and again in my own community: families working hard, earning good money, and still ending up with nothing to show for it generations later — because no one ever taught them how money is supposed to *move*. Not hoarded. Not feared. Moved, on purpose, toward protection and ownership.

That question became a mission. Today, I serve as Co-Founder and CFO of Open Doors Global Development Foundation, Inc., a 501(c)(3) nonprofit built around workforce development, financial literacy, and mentorship. I also lead Open Doors Financial Group (ODFG), where we're building an independent insurance distribution platform designed to give agents — and the families they serve — a better, more transparent path forward. Alongside that, Kingdom Brothers Development is where we turn the same wealth-building philosophy into brick, mortar, and generational real estate.

I hold a Florida 2-14 Life license and work primarily in protection strategy — indexed universal life, whole life, term, and fixed indexed annuities — always with one filter: does this actually serve the family in front of me, or does it just serve a sale? I also compete as an amateur physique athlete, most notably at the Arnold Amateur Championships — a discipline that keeps me honest about the difference between motivation and consistency.

I'm based in Lakeland, Florida, and everything I build — the Foundation, ODFG, Kingdom Brothers — is meant to work together as one ecosystem: a set of open doors that anyone willing to walk through them can use to build something that lasts.
