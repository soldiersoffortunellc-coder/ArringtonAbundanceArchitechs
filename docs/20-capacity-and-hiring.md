# Capacity and Hiring Requirements

## Functions required (mapped to the 6 subordinate agent roles)

| Function | Days 1-30 | Days 31-90 (target scenario, 75 total clients) |
|---|---|---|
| Sales (discovery/demo/close) | Owner-led or 1 dedicated closer | 1-2 closers, depending on lead volume from Days 31-60 campaigns |
| Fulfillment/implementation | 1 implementation specialist (can be owner initially) | 2-3 specialists once concurrent onboarding load exceeds ~5-8 active builds at a time |
| AI/voice prompt engineering | Shared with implementation early on | Dedicated as industry-pack count or client volume grows |
| Client success | Folded into fulfillment initially | Dedicated once live client count exceeds ~15-20 |
| Compliance review (insurance/real estate) | Owner or contracted compliance reviewer, per-client | Same — do not scale this past a qualified reviewer's real capacity |

## Capacity math (illustrative, recalibrate from pilot data)

If one implementation specialist can fully onboard 2 premium-tier clients
or 5 standardized-tier clients per week (an assumption — replace with the
Days 15-30 pilot's actual time-to-launch figure), the target scenario's
25 premium + 50 standardized clients over roughly 10 sellable weeks
(Days 31-90 minus onboarding lag) implies needing 2-3 concurrent
implementation specialists by Day 60 to avoid becoming the bottleneck
identified as "Implementation capacity" on the Executive Command Center.

## Hiring triggers (don't hire ahead of signal)

- Add a second closer when discovery-call volume exceeds what one rep can
  book demos from within the pipeline's target cycle time.
- Add implementation capacity when the `Onboarding`/`Implementation`
  pipeline stages hold more active clients than current capacity can
  clear within the target time-to-launch.
- Add a dedicated client success role when active `LIVE`/`OPTIMIZATION`
  client count makes proactive risk-signal follow-up (`docs/14-client-
  success-workflow.md`) impossible for the fulfillment team to also own.

## Constraint

Per the brief: never sacrifice compliance, delivery quality, or retention
for short-term sales velocity — a hiring gap in fulfillment or compliance
review capacity is a reason to slow sales intake, not skip QA or
compliance review.
