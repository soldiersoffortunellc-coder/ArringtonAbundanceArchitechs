# Sales Follow-Up Sequences

Cadences trigger recommended next actions for the sales team — the system
never sends a commitment or fact it wasn't given.

## Post-discovery, no demo booked yet (7-day sequence)

Day 0: recap email (their stated problem, what demo will show). Day 2:
value-add content touch (relevant case study or educational content, if
available). Day 5: direct ask to book. Day 7: break-up email ("should I
close this out or is timing just off?") → routes to Nurture if no
response.

## Post-demo, no proposal request yet (5-day sequence)

Day 0: recap + proposal delivery. Day 2: check-in on questions. Day 4:
address most common objection proactively (from
`sales-assets/objection-handling-library.md`). Day 5: direct ask for
decision timeline.

## Post-proposal, no verbal commitment (10-day sequence)

Day 0: proposal sent confirmation. Day 3: check-in. Day 6: urgency touch
(only if a genuine, disclosed reason exists — e.g., current cohort/
onboarding capacity — never a fabricated scarcity claim). Day 10: direct
ask; if no response, move to Nurture with loss reason `unresponsive`
pending, not yet `Closed Lost`.

## Database reactivation (dormant prospects, 90+ days no activity)

Monthly touch with new value (case study, new feature, seasonal
relevance) — routes any re-engagement back to Contacted, not a new lead
record, to preserve source/history.

## Rule

Every sequence step is a recommended action for a human or a templated
send — never a fabricated commitment, price, or promise not already
approved for that prospect.
