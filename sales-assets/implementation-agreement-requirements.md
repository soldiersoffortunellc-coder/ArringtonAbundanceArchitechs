# Implementation Agreement — Requirements (not a drafted agreement)

**Per the brief: "Do not publish legal agreements without attorney
review." This file lists what the agreement must cover — it is not legal
language and must not be used as a contract until an attorney drafts or
reviews it.**

## Required sections

1. Parties and effective date.
2. Scope of services — tier purchased, inclusions (pull from
   `config/offers/offer-ladder.json` once approved), explicitly excluded
   items.
3. Fees and payment terms — setup fee, monthly fee, billing cadence,
   refund policy, late-payment terms.
4. Term and termination — minimum term (if any), cancellation notice
   period, what happens to client data on termination (references
   `docs/12-client-onboarding-system.md` safe-offboarding sequence).
5. Data ownership and portability — client owns their contact/business
   data; export process on termination.
6. Ownership of custom work product (AI prompts, custom workflows built
   for this client) — define whether these are licensed for this client's
   use only or transferable.
7. Compliance responsibilities — client's responsibility to supply
   accurate licensing, disclosure, and compensation information (per the
   relevant industry-pack's compliance-review-controls); explicit
   statement that this system does not constitute legal, financial,
   licensing, or lending advice.
8. Performance disclaimers — explicit statement that revenue/lead/
   appointment projections are estimates, not guarantees (ties to
   `docs/21-risks-and-assumptions.md`).
9. Confidentiality and data security.
10. Liability limitation and indemnification.
11. Dispute resolution / governing law.
12. Signature block.

## Process

Draft from these requirements only with a licensed attorney in the
relevant jurisdiction(s); no version of this document is sent to a
prospect until that review is complete and the owner approves it.
