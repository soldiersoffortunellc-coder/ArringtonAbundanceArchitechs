# Case-Study Framework

## Prerequisite (non-negotiable)

Documented client permission AND compliance approval before any client
name, logo, quote, or specific result is published — see
`docs/14-client-success-workflow.md`. No case study is drafted "in
advance" of that permission.

## Structure

1. **Client snapshot** — industry, size, location (only what the client
   approved for disclosure).
2. **The problem** — in the client's own words where possible (direct
   quote, approved).
3. **What was implemented** — which tier, which components (factual, from
   their actual build).
4. **The result** — only figures the client has confirmed and approved for
   publication (e.g., "reduced missed-call rate from X% to Y%," "recovered
   Z estimated additional jobs/month") — sourced from their own
   Executive Command Center data, never modeled/projected figures.
5. **Client quote** — approved verbatim, not paraphrased into something
   stronger.
6. **Disclosure line** — "Results are specific to this client and are not
   guaranteed for every business" on every published case study.

## Where used

Landing pages (as proof strip once available), webinar presentations,
proposal exhibits, referral-partner enablement.
