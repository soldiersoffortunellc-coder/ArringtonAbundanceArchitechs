# Client Onboarding Checklist (client-facing)

Sent immediately after `AGREEMENT_CONFIRMED`. Mirrors
`docs/12-client-onboarding-system.md` but written for the client, not the
internal team.

## What we need from you

- [ ] Business legal name, physical address, and logo/brand assets
- [ ] Domain access (or DNS delegation) for your client-facing pages
- [ ] Phone number(s) — new number provisioning, or existing number
      port/forwarding authorization
- [ ] Email sending domain access (for deliverability setup)
- [ ] Team member list: name, email, role, phone
- [ ] Calendar/availability rules for booking
- [ ] Services offered / service area (home services) or license states /
      carrier appointments (insurance) or MLS/market areas (real estate)
- [ ] Pricing or compensation structure to reference in AI conversations
      (never invented on your behalf)
- [ ] Required disclosures (compliance/legal-supplied)
- [ ] Existing integrations to connect (financing partner, review
      platforms, etc.)
- [ ] Escalation contact for urgent client/lead issues

## What happens on our side (for visibility)

Snapshot selection → subaccount setup → configuration → integration
testing → your live walkthrough (`CLIENT_REVIEW`) → your launch approval →
live. See `docs/12-client-onboarding-system.md` for the full sequence.

## Your launch approval

Nothing goes live without your explicit sign-off at the `CLIENT_REVIEW` →
`LAUNCH_APPROVED` step.
