# Industry-Specific Landing Page Outlines

Each outline is a section-by-section structure for a one-page funnel
landing page. Copywriting should pull directly from the matching offer
sheet (`sales-assets/offer-sheets.md`) and industry-pack doc — never
invent claims or statistics not sourced from the client or this repo.

## Structure (shared across all three)

1. **Headline** — the named problem, not the product ("Stop losing jobs to
   missed calls" not "Try our CRM").
2. **Subhead** — who it's for (owner-operator language, specific to the
   vertical).
3. **Proof strip** — logos/credentials if available; omit if not (never
   fabricate social proof).
4. **The problem, in their words** — 3-4 bullets matching the "Problems
   this pack solves" list from the relevant industry-pack doc.
5. **The fix, in outcomes** — 4-6 bullets matching "Required components,"
   phrased as outcomes not features (e.g., "Never miss another call" not
   "Missed-call text-back workflow").
6. **How it works** — 3-step visual: Install → Configure → Live in
   [time-to-launch].
7. **CTA** — book a discovery call (routes to the discovery-call
   calendar); form fields match `sales-assets/qualification-form.md`.
8. **FAQ** — pricing withheld until approved (see offer sheet note);
   answer timeline, compliance, and "do I need to change my number"
   questions directly.
9. **Final CTA.**

## Industry-specific headline options (draft, pick and test)

- **Insurance:** "Recruit licensed agents on autopilot — and never lose
  another one to silence." / "Your agency's recruiting pipeline,
  automated."
- **Real Estate:** "Respond to every lead in under 60 seconds — even at
  2am." / "Your database is a goldmine. Start mining it."
- **Home Services:** "Never miss another call — or another job." /
  "Turn every unsold estimate into a booked job."

## Compliance note (applies to all three)

Every landing page must carry the correct TCPA consent language on its
form and, for real estate, Fair Housing–compliant copy — see the relevant
industry-pack doc's `complianceReviewControls`.
