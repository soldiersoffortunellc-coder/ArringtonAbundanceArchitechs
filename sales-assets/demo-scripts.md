# Demo Scripts

15-20 minute structure, adapted per industry. Never demo a feature the
client's tier doesn't include (`config/offers/offer-ladder.json`), and
never state a result as guaranteed (see `docs/21-risks-and-assumptions.md`).

## Universal demo structure

1. **Open (2 min)** — confirm the one or two problems surfaced in
   discovery (`sales-assets/discovery-call-script.md` notes). Do not
   re-run a generic pitch — reference their specific answers.
2. **Show the missed-call/instant-response flow live (3 min)** — call or
   text the demo number, show the automatic response land in real time.
   This is the single highest-impact demo moment across all three
   industries.
3. **Show the relevant pipeline (5 min)** — walk one contact through the
   industry pipeline stage-by-stage (Buyer pipeline for real estate,
   Service Request for home services, Recruiting pipeline for insurance).
4. **Show one AI conversation exchange (3 min)** — a realistic scripted
   exchange with the AI Conversation Agent relevant to their vertical.
5. **Show the dashboard (2 min)** — the industry activity dashboard,
   framed around the metric that matters most to them (production for
   insurance, pipeline velocity for real estate, estimate conversion for
   home services).
6. **Close (3-5 min)** — recap the 2-3 problems solved, transition to
   proposal (`sales-assets/proposal-template.md`), confirm next step and
   timeline.

## Industry-specific must-show moments

- **Insurance:** candidate scoring in action; no-show recovery on an
  interview; inactive-agent reactivation sequence.
- **Real Estate:** showing follow-up automation; home-valuation funnel
  intake to first-touch; database reactivation on a sample cold contact.
- **Home Services:** emergency-service routing decision; unsold-estimate
  follow-up; maintenance-reminder cadence.

## Guardrails

- Use only the client's own data or clearly-marked demo/sample data —
  never another client's real information.
- If asked about pricing before it's approved for publication, redirect to
  "I'll have exact numbers in your proposal" rather than quoting a draft
  range.
