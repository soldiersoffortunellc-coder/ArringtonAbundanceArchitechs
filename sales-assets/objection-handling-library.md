# Objection-Handling Library

Answer honestly; never fabricate a stat or claim a guaranteed result to
overcome an objection.

## "It's too expensive"

Reframe to cost-of-inaction using their own numbers from the ROI
calculator (`sales-assets/roi-calculator.md`) — what is a single missed
call/lead/recruit actually worth to them today. Offer the lower tier
(Starter SaaS) if budget is the real constraint rather than discounting
the proposed tier ad hoc.

## "I already have a CRM"

Acknowledge directly — this is explicitly not a generic CRM. Ask what
their current tool does for missed-call recovery, AI qualification, and
industry-specific pipelines; most incumbent CRMs do none of the three.

## "I've been burned by automation/AI before"

Ask what specifically failed. Most failures trace to generic, non-
industry-specific setups. Point to the industry-pack compliance controls
and the QA checklist (`docs/13-fulfillment-workflow.md`) as the difference.

## "I don't have time to implement this"

Point to the onboarding checklist — the client's actual time investment is
mostly a few intake items, not ongoing labor; the fulfillment team does
the configuration work.

## "How do I know this will work for my business specifically?"

Never promise a guaranteed result. Offer: (1) the ROI calculator using
their own numbers, (2) a case study if one exists in their industry
(`sales-assets/case-study-framework.md`), (3) the QA process that
validates the system works correctly before launch.

## "What if I want to cancel?"

Point to the term/cancellation terms in the (attorney-reviewed)
Implementation Agreement and the safe-offboarding process
(`docs/12-client-onboarding-system.md`) — data export guaranteed, no
lock-in beyond the stated term.

## Insurance-specific: "Is this compliant with state recruiting rules?"

Point to the compliance-review-controls checklist
(`docs/06-industry-pack-insurance.md`) and that no insurance client goes
live without their own compliance contact signing off.
