# Referral-Partner Campaign

## Target partner types by industry

- **Insurance:** FMOs/IMOs, insurance CE course providers, other agency
  principals not in direct competition.
- **Real Estate:** mortgage brokers, title companies, home inspectors,
  brokerages' training directors.
- **Home Services:** complementary trades (e.g., HVAC ↔ electrical),
  trade associations, franchisors, equipment suppliers/distributors.

## Structure

1. **Identify** — partners with a existing relationship to the target
   buyer and no direct competitive conflict.
2. **Pitch the partner, not the end client** — the pitch is "your network
   gets infrastructure that makes them more successful (and more able to
   pay you)," not a generic co-marketing ask.
3. **Referral mechanics** — unique tracked referral link/code
   (`referral_partner_id` attribution field from the universal snapshot);
   commission or reciprocal-referral structure to be defined by the CRO
   before any partner agreement is signed (pricing/commission is
   configurable, not published here).
4. **Enablement** — give partners the relevant offer sheet
   (`sales-assets/offer-sheets.md`) and a short explainer, not raw access
   to internal pricing config.
5. **Recognition** — a partner leaderboard/dashboard view showing
   referrals sent and closed, feeding the "results by acquisition source"
   Executive Command Center view.

## Compliance note

Any paid-referral arrangement must be reviewed for state-specific referral-
fee rules, especially in insurance and real estate — do not activate a
commission-based referral program without that review.
