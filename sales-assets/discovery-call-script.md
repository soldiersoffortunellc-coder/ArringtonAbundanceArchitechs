# Discovery-Call Script

Goal: qualify + surface the specific, costly problem to build the demo and
proposal around. 15-20 minutes.

## Opening (2 min)

"Thanks for booking time. Before I show you anything, I want to understand
what's actually costing you money right now — that way I only show you
what's relevant to your business."

## Qualification questions (map directly to qualification form fields)

1. What's your biggest bottleneck right now — leads, follow-up,
   recruiting/staffing, or something else?
2. Roughly how many [leads/calls/recruits] come in per week, and what
   happens to them today?
3. What's your average [ticket size / commission / production value] on a
   closed [job/deal/agent]?
4. Who else needs to be involved in a decision like this?
5. What's your timeline — is this a "fix now" problem or a "someday"
   problem?
6. What have you tried before, and why didn't it work?

## Urgency and budget signals (do not state as fact — note as signal)

- Ability-to-pay signal: current team size, current software spend, stated
  growth plans.
- Urgency signal: language like "we're losing deals every week" vs.
  "would be nice to fix."

## Close

"Based on what you've told me, here's what I'd want to show you: [1-2
specific components]. Let's get 20 minutes on the calendar to show you
exactly how that would work in your business." → book demo.

## Hard rule

Never promise a specific revenue outcome, close rate, or ROI figure on
this call — those are modeled targets (`docs/04-financial-model.md`), not
guarantees for any individual prospect. Use the ROI calculator
(`sales-assets/roi-calculator.md`) with their own numbers instead of a
generic promise.
