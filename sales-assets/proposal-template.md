# Proposal Template

## Structure

1. **Cover** — client name, date, prepared by.
2. **The problem you told us** — 2-3 bullets, direct quotes/paraphrases
   from the discovery call. No generic industry pain points.
3. **What we recommend** — the specific offer tier and inclusions from
   `config/offers/offer-ladder.json` (only after `status: APPROVED`).
4. **Your estimated impact** — output of the ROI calculator
   (`sales-assets/roi-calculator.md`) using their own numbers, with the
   recovery-rate assumption shown.
5. **What's included** — full inclusion list for the proposed tier.
6. **Implementation timeline** — reference the onboarding state machine
   stages in plain language (intake → configuration → QA → launch), with
   the average time-to-launch figure once real data exists
   (`docs/12-client-onboarding-system.md`); until then, state a target
   range and label it as a target, not a guarantee.
7. **Investment** — setup fee + monthly fee (approved pricing only).
8. **What we need from you** — credentials/access checklist
   (`sales-assets/onboarding-checklist.md` preview).
9. **Next steps** — signature, payment, kickoff date.
10. **Terms reference** — "Full terms in the Implementation Agreement" —
    do not embed agreement legal language directly in the proposal; see
    `sales-assets/implementation-agreement-requirements.md`.

## Hard rules

- No modeled 90-day revenue figures appear in an individual client
  proposal — those are internal planning targets, not this client's
  promised outcome.
- No feature is listed that the proposed tier does not actually include.
